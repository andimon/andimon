Hi, I am Andre Vella

I'm a backend engineer who works mostly in Elixir and Phoenix, building real-money systems for the online gaming industry. Since mid-2025 I've helped the team to build a crypto wallet platform from the ground up: passkey and recovery-key authentication, hot and cold wallet handling, multi-chain support and exchange integrations.

I later moved to the core game-aggregation platform, where I worked on a number of items such as building fraud notification pipelines over RabbitMQ that link game suppliers to operators. I've also made the currency layer faster and more reliable with in-memory caching, kept credentials out of logs, and tracked down performance problems in high-traffic services.

I take my own changes all the way to production, rolling them out across regions with infrastructure-as-code and adding metrics so I can see they work. I care about code that explains itself, tests that each check one thing, and error messages that say why something failed, not just that it did.

With AI tools, code is written faster than ever. I use them every day, and that is exactly why I spend more of my time reading, analysing and understanding, so what we ship stays consistent and correct.
